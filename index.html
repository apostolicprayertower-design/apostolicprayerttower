About Our Church

Apostolic Prayer Tower was founded on June 3, 2003, through the divine vision entrusted to our revered founder, Late Rev. Ramanaduala Azariah Prabhakar Garu. With a heart deeply burdened for the unreached, he stepped out in faith to proclaim the Gospel in the suburbs of Hyderabad, focusing especially on the regions of Jodimetla, Chowdariguda, and Ghatkesar. His mission was clear: to reach souls who had not yet heard the saving message of Christ and to establish a prayer-centered community.

In its earliest days, the ministry began with one faithful family, serving those from humble and struggling backgrounds. The journey was marked by intense opposition, criticism, and spiritual resistance, yet the church stood firm, relying solely on God’s abundant grace and miraculous guidance. Through fervent prayer and steadfast faith, the congregation grew steadily, blossoming into a fellowship of 25 committed families, each a living testimony of God’s transforming power.

Over the years, Apostolic Prayer Tower became more than a congregation—it became a spiritual refuge, a place of healing, restoration, and empowerment. Prayer was the heartbeat of the ministry, and every breakthrough was attributed to the Lord’s hand at work.

“Not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit, says the Lord” — Zechariah 4:6

On June 30, 2017, our beloved founder and Bishop was called home to be with the Lord. His passing was a moment of deep sorrow, yet his vision and legacy continued to shine brightly. In faith and obedience, Bishopamma graciously assumed the mantle of leadership, ensuring that the mission did not falter.

Alongside her, their son, Rev. Ramanadula Abhishek Preetham—who was anointed in 2015 for ministry—stepped into active service, carrying forward the torch of prayer, evangelism, and pastoral care.

Today, Apostolic Prayer Tower stands as a living testimony of God’s faithfulness. From humble beginnings to a thriving ministry, it continues to shine as a beacon of hope in the community, proclaiming Christ’s unchanging love and building lives through prayer. The legacy of our founder lives on through the leadership of Bishopamma and Rev. Abhishek Preetham, as the church presses forward with renewed vision to reach the unreached and disciple believers for the glory of God.
