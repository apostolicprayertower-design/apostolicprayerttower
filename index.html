APOSTOLIC PRAYER TOWER

“Prayer our foundation, souls our mission, Christ our vision.”

Vision Statement

To establish Christ-centered communities across the nations, where churches become living sanctuaries of worship, fellowship, and service. Each community will embody the love of Christ through prayer, education, care for the orphaned and aged, healing through hospitals, equipping through Bible colleges and seminaries, and hospitality through pilgrimage homes. Our vision is to prepare generations in the Word, nurture them spiritually, and lead multitudes to Christ as we prepare the way for the Lord.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to build and multiply community churches that reveal Christ’s love in action. Through integrated spaces of worship, pastoral care, dining fellowship, schools, hospitals, orphanages, homes for the aged, and training centers for discipleship, we seek to lead souls to salvation, equip believers in Scripture, strengthen families, and serve humanity with compassion. Apostolic Prayer Tower is committed to raising spiritually mature generations who will glorify God and carry forward the Great Commission until His return.

About Apostolic Prayer Tower

Foundational Years

Apostolic Prayer Tower was founded on June 3, 2003, through the divine vision entrusted to our revered founder, Late Rev. Ramanadula Azariah Prabhakar Garu. With a heart burdened for the unreached, he stepped out in faith to proclaim the Gospel in the suburbs of Hyderabad, focusing especially on Jodimetla, Pillaipalli, Pedaamberpet, Yadigirigutta, Chikatmammidi, Bhongiri, Pocharam, and Singapore Township.

The ministry began with one faithful family, serving those from humble and struggling backgrounds. Despite intense opposition and spiritual resistance, the church stood firm, relying solely on God’s abundant grace and miraculous guidance. Through fervent prayer and steadfast faith, the congregation grew steadily, blossoming into a fellowship of 25 committed families, each a living testimony of God’s transforming power. Apostolic Prayer Tower became a spiritual refuge, a place of healing, restoration, and empowerment, with prayer as its heartbeat and every breakthrough attributed to the Lord’s hand at work.

Transition Years

On June 30, 2017, our beloved founder was called home to be with the Lord. His passing was a moment of deep sorrow, yet his vision and legacy continued to shine brightly. With faith and courage, Bishopamma Mrs. Maddela Kalavathy Prabhakar stepped into leadership, ensuring that the mission did not falter.

Alongside her, their son, Rev. Ramanadula Abhishek Preetham, who was anointed in 2015 for ministry, took active charge of the church. Together, Bishopamma Mrs. Maddela Kalavathy Prabhakar and Rev. Ramanadula Abhishek Preetham have strengthened the ministry, expanding its reach while remaining faithful to its founding vision: to build lives through prayer and proclaim Christ’s unchanging love.

Today

Apostolic Prayer Tower stands as a living testimony of God’s faithfulness. From humble beginnings to a thriving ministry, it continues to shine as a beacon of hope in the community, proclaiming the Gospel, discipling believers, and raising generations rooted in prayer. The legacy of Late Rev. Ramanadula Azariah Prabhakar Garu lives on through the leadership of Bishopamma Mrs. Maddela Kalavathy Prabhakar and Rev. Ramanadula Abhishek Preetham, as the church presses forward with renewed vision to reach the unreached and glorify Christ.
